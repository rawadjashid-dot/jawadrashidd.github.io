About

I am an electrical engineer interested in computer vision, edge AI and reinforcement learning.

Most of my research so far is on visual inspection of printed circuit boards. With my collaborators, I have built datasets from physically fabricated boards, released as PCB-Defect and DefectDet, and designed detectors for small, oriented surface defects, such as P2LK-YOLO.

I have also built hardware from the ground up: an 8-bit computer, a two-player game made only from logic ICs, and a PID-controlled ball balancer. You can find these on the Projects page.
